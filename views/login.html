Privacy notice — how we use your information

OLV Water Refilling Station collects your name, contact number, email, delivery address, and, if you choose to save it, your home location. We use them only to take and deliver your orders, contact you about them, send your receipts, and keep sales and delivery records.

Only station staff and our delivery drivers can see your details. We do not sell or share them with anyone else. Records are kept on the station's computer for as long as you are our customer and as required for business records.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by calling or messaging the station at +63 919 841 7627 or +63 968 531 0277. You can also close your account from your account settings.
